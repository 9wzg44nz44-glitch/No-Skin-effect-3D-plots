Path model (all scenarios share the same transmitter enclosure):
Copper Faraday cage ⅛ in (3.175 mm) → 33 ft air → 3 in deep-submergence steel → submerged under 100 m seawater.
Because the longitudinal / potential waves experience no classical skin effect, none of these layers contribute exponential attenuation; only pure geometric 1/r spherical spreading is used from the effective radiating tip to the detector.

Detector locations:
• Surface Ship — detector at horizontal distance r on the ocean surface (wave travels through the enclosure + 100 m seawater + remaining horizontal path).
• Land — detector on land; path continues through earth crust and mountains after leaving the seawater.
• Airplane / Satellite — detector elevated; after leaving the seawater the wave propagates through air then outer space.
